# Incident Reports

Incident reports document completed investigations in a consistent format.

## Report Template

### Summary
Short description of the activity.

### Detection
What generated the investigation.

### Timeline
Relevant events in chronological order.

### Evidence
Important SIEM results, endpoint events and screenshots.

### Analysis
What the available telemetry shows.

### Impact
Observed or confirmed impact within the lab.

### Response
Actions taken during the investigation.

### Detection Gap
What was not visible or what failed to trigger.

### Lessons Learned
What should be changed in the lab.

### Status
Open / Closed / Inconclusive.

Do not claim compromise or impact unless the collected evidence supports it.
